About

Receipt Tracker AI scans receipts using Apple's built-in document scanner, extracts data with on-device Vision OCR, and categorizes expenses automatically. The app is 100% offline for free users, with an optional server-powered AI layer for paid subscribers.

  • Platform: iOS 17+
  • Pricing: Free with optional Pro and AI Insights subscriptions
