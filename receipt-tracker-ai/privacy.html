Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

Receipt Tracker AI is built privacy-first. This policy describes what data the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

TL;DR

  • Free tier: All processing happens on your device. Your receipt data never leaves your phone.
  • AI Insights tier (optional, paid): Only merchant names are sent to our server when you explicitly trigger smart categorization or insights. Receipt totals, dates, and line items stay on-device.
  • No tracking. We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • No account required. The app works fully offline.

Data We Store On Your Device

The following data is stored locally in Apple SwiftData on your iPhone:

DataPurposeStored Where
Receipt images (photos, scans)For your records and re-reviewApp Documents directory
Extracted text (merchant, date, total, line items)Receipt detailsSwiftData database
Category assignment (business/personal, category code)Tax export & dashboardSwiftData database
Onboarding state, category profile, display preferencesRemember your settingsUserDefaults

None of this data is sent anywhere by default. It remains on your device until you delete it (Settings → Delete All Data) or export it yourself (Settings → Export All Data as JSON).

Optional AI Insights (Paid Tier)

If you subscribe to AI Insights (Monthly or Annual), the app may contact our server to provide:

  • Smart categorization — sends the merchant name only.
  • Monthly spending summary — sends merchant names, totals, and category codes for the month being summarized.
  • Tax optimization suggestions — sends year-to-date category totals and receipt counts (no merchant names, no individual receipt details).

Authentication: Every server request includes your StoreKit 2 purchase token (JWS) to verify your subscription. This token is validated server-side and not used for tracking or advertising.

The server does NOT receive:

  • Receipt images
  • Full line-item details
  • Your personal name, email, or contact info
  • Precise locations
  • Any data from the free tier

The AI Insights feature is opt-in by subscription. If you do not subscribe, no network requests are made.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No analytics. We do not use analytics SDKs (no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude). We cannot see what you tap, when you open the app, or what you scan.
  • No advertising identifiers. We do not access IDFA or any advertising identifier.
  • No crash reporting that identifies you. If you opt into Apple's built-in crash reporting via iOS Settings, that is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.
  • No third-party SDKs. The app uses only Apple frameworks (SwiftUI, SwiftData, Vision, VisionKit, StoreKit).

Data Storage & Security

  • All on-device data is protected by iOS's built-in data protection (NSFileProtectionComplete for receipt images).
  • Server requests (AI Insights only) use HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • The server stores no persistent user data; requests are processed in memory and discarded.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access: Open the app — all your data is visible to you.
  • Export: Settings → Export All Data (JSON) creates a portable backup.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete All Data permanently removes all receipts and images from your device.
  • Unsubscribe: Manage Subscription in Settings or via the App Store app.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. Receipt tracking is a general productivity use case.

International Users

Because all free-tier data stays on your device, GDPR and CCPA data-residency concerns do not apply to the free tier. For AI Insights subscribers, server processing is transient and no personal data is retained.

Changes to This Policy

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes and in this document's "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/thecliffhanger/receipt-tracker-ai or email support@receipttracker.ai.
