AI Research & Builds

Independent, from-scratch AI systems research - designed, trained, and benchmarked on my own hardware

A System-1 typed-decision judge, built from scratch

An original decision model that evaluates AI agent traces - tool calls, tool outputs, final answers - and returns typed judgments with calibrated probabilities. Original decision-head architecture and training pipeline over a BERT-family encoder, trained end-to-end on a MacBook Pro on locally generated synthetic data. Open source under Apache-2.0 with reproducible training and evaluation scripts.

99.7% macro accuracy across 5 agent-trace judgment families on 2,500 held-out test cases - at 24ms latency, $0 per judgment, and bit-exact determinism (0.0 probability deviation over 100 repeated inferences): properties text-generating LLM judges cannot match. Also published the five-layer trust benchmark - schema conformance, calibration under out-of-distribution rules, determinism, perturbation invariance, pinned reproducibility - run against both the model and GPT-class LLM-judge baselines, with honest results either way.

The Context Layer thesis

Enterprise AI needs its own operating system: a governed context and retrieval layer between models and enterprise data. Delivered as a solo talk to 12,000+ attendees at AI4 2026.

The Confidence Gap thesis

AI output trustworthiness is an engineering problem solved by layered validation: type conformance, confidence calibration, contract validation, and observability. Solo talk, PyTorch Conference NA 2026 Responsible AI track.

Who evaluates the evaluator

Evaluation systems must themselves be evaluated - for calibration, determinism, and cost. A recursion the industry is converging on through cheap, typed System-1 judge models; subject of forthcoming applied research.
