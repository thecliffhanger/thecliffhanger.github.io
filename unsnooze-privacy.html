UnSnooze Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 28, 2026

Overview

UnSnooze does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Period.

What we DON'T do

  • No data collection — We don't collect any personal information.
  • No analytics — We don't track how you use the app.
  • No ads — Zero third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No accounts — You don't sign up for anything.
  • No servers — The app doesn't communicate with any server.
  • No location — We don't access your location.
  • No contacts — We don't access your contacts.

What stays on your device

All alarm data (times, labels, preferences) is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

Permissions used

  • Notifications — To deliver alarm notifications at the scheduled time.
  • Audio — To play alarm sounds even when the device is silent or locked.

Apple & App Store

Apple may collect data through App Store services (purchase history, device analytics) as described in their own privacy policy. This is outside our control.

Children's Privacy

UnSnooze is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app changes. Any updates will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.
